Property & Asset Management

Comprehensive solution for managing properties, assets, and their assignments

Overview

The Property & Asset Management module extends Odoo 19 Inventory to provide a complete solution for managing physical properties (buildings, meeting rooms, offices) and trackable assets (monitors, projectors, furniture, equipment) within your organization.

Key Features

Property Management

  • Buildings, floors, rooms hierarchy
  • Meeting rooms & conference halls
  • Offices & workspaces
  • Server rooms & storage
  • Custom property types

Asset Tracking

  • IT equipment (computers, monitors)
  • Projectors & AV equipment
  • Furniture (desks, chairs)
  • Serial number tracking
  • Depreciation calculation

Assignment Management

  • Assign assets to properties
  • Track custody & responsibility
  • Assignment history
  • Bulk assignment wizard
  • Return tracking

Maintenance

  • Preventive maintenance scheduling
  • Corrective maintenance
  • Maintenance requests
  • Cost tracking
  • Service vendor management

Property Types Included

  • Building - Physical building structures
  • Floor - Building levels
  • Office - Office spaces
  • Meeting Room - Meeting/Conference rooms
  • Conference Hall - Large event halls
  • Training Room - Workshop rooms
  • Server Room - Data centers
  • Storage Room - Storage facilities
  • Reception Area - Lobby areas
  • Cafeteria - Break rooms

Asset Categories Included

  • IT Equipment - Computers, Monitors, Printers, Projectors, Network Equipment
  • Furniture - Desks, Chairs, Cabinets
  • AV Equipment - Display Screens, Sound Systems, Cameras
  • Appliances - Office appliances
  • HVAC Equipment - Heating/Cooling systems
  • Safety Equipment - Security devices
Multi-Company Support: Full support for multi-company environments with proper access control and data isolation.

Reports

  • Property Assets Report - List all assets assigned to a property
  • Asset Report - Detailed asset information with history
  • Assignment Report - Printable assignment form with signatures

Technical Details

  • Compatible with Odoo 19 Enterprise/Community
  • Extends Product Template for asset management
  • Integrates with Stock/Inventory module
  • Full mail thread support (chatter)
  • Activity management
